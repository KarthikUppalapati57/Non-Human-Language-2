During this session, the dolphins were predominantly engaged in play, which accounted for a significant 78% of their total activity. This high level of social engagement is corroborated by the vocalization data, which shows a prevalence of whistles (49.2%) and burst-pulsed sounds (11.9%) typically associated with social excitement and communication. The frequent use of echolocation clicks further suggests that this play was dynamic, likely involving the active investigation of objects or conspecifics within their environment.
